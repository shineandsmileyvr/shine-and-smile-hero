Before your appointment • Gently brush and floss. • Use a desensitizing toothpaste for a week beforehand. Our favourites: Colgate Sensitive Pro-Relief and Sensodyne Rapid Relief. After your appointment • Skip dark coloured foods and drinks for at least 24 hours, ideally 48. • If you do have dark drinks, use a straw. • Keep using desensitizing toothpaste for a day or two if sensitivity is a concern. How does in-office whitening work? Hydrogen peroxide opens the pores of the teeth and breaks down stains from the inside, and a dental LED light activates and enhances the process for longer lasting results. Most clients see teeth two to six shades lighter after a single treatment, with zero to minimal sensitivity. Does teeth whitening hurt? Most clients feel little to no discomfort. Some notice temporary sensitivity during or after, especially with naturally sensitive teeth. It is usually mild and goes away within 24 to 48 hours. Is it safe? Will it damage my enamel? Yes, it is safe. Hydrogen peroxide and carbamide peroxide are well studied. Done properly, professional whitening removes stains without damaging healthy enamel. Any sensitivity is temporary and subsides after a day or two. How white will my teeth get, and how many sessions will I need? Results depend on your starting shade, the type of staining and your enamel, and no provider can guarantee a number of shades. Most clients see dramatic results after one session. Heavy stains may benefit from a mini touch-up 2 to 4 weeks later. How long does it last? It depends on oral hygiene, diet, smoking and how much coffee, tea and red wine you enjoy. Many clients enjoy their results for several months to a few years, with occasional touch-ups every 6 to 12 months. Am I a good candidate? Most adults with healthy teeth and gums are. Whitening is not recommended for anyone under 16, pregnant, or in braces. Regular dental cleanings help results last. Can I whiten crowns, veneers, bonding or fillings? No. Whitening only affects natural enamel, so restorations will not change colour. It can bring the surrounding natural teeth closer to the colour of past restorative work. I have an event coming up. When should I come in? At least one week before, so the pores can close and the tones in your teeth can even out. How is in-office whitening different from over-the-counter products? In-office whitening uses professional-grade products applied by trained technicians, with a barrier to protect your gums, for faster and longer lasting results.
