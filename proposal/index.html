What’s holding your current site back

Slow on phones

Google’s Lighthouse test, on a simulated phone, scores your home page 14 out of 100. The Instagram feed is the heaviest part of the page.

Speed score 14/100Main content shows after ~15.8 sPage size 4.5 MB

The page jumps while loading

Text and buttons move as images arrive, so it’s easy to tap the wrong thing.

Booking is hard to find on phones

There’s no Book button in the phone header and no call or book bar that stays on screen.

No reviews on the site

Visitors can’t see what your clients say without leaving to search for you.

Small before and after photos

Your best proof, your real results, is shown small and easy to scroll past.

Google basics missing

No main headline on the home page, 7 pages with no title or description, 4 duplicate course pages, and no business info for your two locations.

Pinch-zoom is blocked

Visitors can’t zoom in on a phone, and some text is low contrast and hard to read.

Small things that look out of date

The Facebook link in your footer is broken, and the footer still says © 2025.
